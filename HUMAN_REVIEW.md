# Human Review and Approval

## Purpose

The GenAR Pharmacovigilance Safety Intelligence Platform generates a structured safety analysis and a PADER-style report from the supplied ICSR dataset.

The generated results are intended to support human review and do not replace clinical, medical, pharmacovigilance, or regulatory expertise.

---

## Human Review Workflow

Before the generated report is considered final, a qualified human reviewer should review the following stages:

```text
Dataset
   ↓
Data Validation
   ↓
Safety Analysis
   ↓
Evidence Generation
   ↓
AI / Report Narrative Generation
   ↓
Human Review
   ↓
Approved Final Report
